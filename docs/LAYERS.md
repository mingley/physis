## The rule

A claim names a layer. A knob names a layer. Worlds are slices across layers. When a spacetime knob changes a particle verdict, that is a cross-layer coupling and must be visible in the diff.

## v0 map

**mathematical.** Type-level SI; Lie groups as enums.

**spacetime.** `Manifold { dim, signature, compact_extra, compact_radius_planck, topology }`. Observed 4D is a claim, not an assumption of the substrate (the substrate *allows* `dim=26`).

**quantum.** Finite kets, Born rule, Pauli. Enough to prove the layer is real. Not QFT.

**field.** Occupied by theory flags (`kind`, `supersymmetry`) until M4.

**particle.** SM catalog. Graviton is `Hypothetical`.

**interaction.** `GaugeGroup` products. SM embedding table.

**effective.** Compactification radius vs collider scales (heuristic).

**statistical.** Empty. Needed for electricity/thermo.

**information.** The journal is the embryo.

**agent.** `Lab`.

## Smallest stuff

Quarks are the smallest *empirically confirmed* constituents in this encoding. They are not point-particle metaphysics; they are species in a spectrum with color and fractional charge. Strings are not smaller quarks. Strings are a different theory of what the spectrum *is*.
